        🤲 Sign Language Channel (you → speech)

        Sign your message, click Stop — all signs are analysed together and spoken as one sentence.

        Supported signs — 3 recognition tiers (hold each ~1 s)

        Tier 1 · MediaPipe pre-trained CNN (7 words)

        • 🖐 Open palm → hello
        • ✊ Closed fist → yes
        • 👍 Thumb up → ok
        • 👇 Thumb down → no
        • ☝️ Point up → you
        • ✌️ Peace / V → peace
        • 🤟 ILY → love

        Tier 2 · ASL letter model (A–Z when loaded)

        • Sign each letter of any word to fingerspell it
        • e.g. spell Y-O-U O-K → LLM → "Are you okay?"

        Tier 3 · Geometric rules (25 extra handshapes)

        • 🤘 Rock-on → more
        • 👋 L-shape → help
        • 🤙 Thumb+pinky → phone
        • ☝️ Middle only → who
        • 🤙 Pinky only → me
        • 3 fingers → three
        • 4 fingers → four
        • Thumb+middle → six
        • Thumb+ring → seven
        • 3+index+pinky → nine
        • index+ring → letter R
        • middle+pinky → letter U
        • ring+pinky → letter H

        The LLM converts the gloss into a natural English sentence, so sign you ok and get "Are you okay?".
